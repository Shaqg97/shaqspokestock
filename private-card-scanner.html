AI card scanning station

How it works: scan a batch of card fronts with your fi-8170 to image files, import them here, and the AI will analyse each image in sequence. You see each photo, AI readout, confidence and catalogue matches as it goes. Confirm uncertain results before listing. The scanner cannot silently access a computer folder; you select the exported scan files in the browser.

1 · Enable free AI image recognition

Create a free Gemini API key in Google AI Studio, then paste it below. We use Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite for image understanding. The key stays in this page's memory only and is not saved to the site, database or browser storage. Images are sent to Google for analysis; Google's free-tier terms say submitted content may be used to improve its products. Do not use this mode for images you do not want sent to Google.

Get free Gemini API key ↗

Free quotas and model access are controlled by Google and can change. This app will not upgrade billing or switch to a paid model automatically. Stop processing if Google reports a quota or billing error.

2 · Import your scanner batch

Use the fi-8170 to save card fronts as JPG/PNG. Select multiple images in one batch. If filenames contain “back”, those images are kept as back-side references and the matching “front” image is analysed. Other images are analysed individually.
