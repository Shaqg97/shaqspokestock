3. Physical storage & SKU guide

For the 2,000-card mixed-storage setup, use a protected hit binder for premium singles, numbered long boxes for standard singles, and labelled lot boxes for bundles and bulk. Always assign a physical location before listing. Never change a SKU just because a card is repriced.

High-value binderH01-P02-04Binder 01 · page 02 · pocket 04
Standard singlesS01-0042Singles box 01 · position 0042
Bundles / bulkL01-0012Lot box 01 · lot 0012
New storageIncrementUse H02, S02, L02; never reuse sold SKUs

SKU field accepts these formats. For an individual listing, use S unless it belongs in the hit binder; for a bundle or bulk lot, use L. Keep standard singles in intake order and search them digitally by name, set, number, language and variant. Full setup and pick/pack instructions: Physical inventory & SKU workflow.

eBay selling & postage strategy

Your selected defaults: mixed individual singles and bundles; buyer pays postage; offer an untracked option only where Australia Post rules allow it, plus a tracked option. Enter the actual amounts you plan to charge and pay for each service below on each card row.

Postage paymentBuyer pays
Listing mixSingles + bundles
Shipping choicesUntracked + tracked
Profit basisAfter costs

Fee defaults are deliberately zero until you enter the fee rate and fixed fee that apply to your account/category. The calculator applies the percentage to item price plus buyer-paid postage, then subtracts card cost, packaging and actual postage. Profit is an estimate before tax, refunds and other overheads. For multi-quantity orders, fixed fee is counted once per listing row; adjust for actual order structure.

CSV export notes

The CSV is a generic listing-preparation sheet with titles, quantity, asking price, condition, SKU and notes. It is not yet certified as a direct eBay Seller Hub bulk-upload template; eBay templates and required fields can change. Review the exported columns against the current eBay AU template before uploading.

No paid services or API keys are required. The catalogue lookup uses the public TCGdex API when you choose to search; if it is unavailable, edit the row manually.
